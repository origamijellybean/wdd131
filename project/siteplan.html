Tyson's Origami website plan

Site Name:

Tyson's Origami

This website helps people learn about origami in general. Eventually I plan on publishing my origami, and maybe putting pictures on a website.

Site Purpose:

This site helps people learn about origami in general, such as different styles, helps them find designs and paper, and also introduces them to my origami.

Scenarios:

  • What is origami?
  • What can I fold?
  • Where can I find paper and designs to fold with?
